WUKKI TV – PRIVACY POLICY

Wukki TV connects directly to the selected M3U playlist, XMLTV guide, and the stream or image providers referenced by them. Those providers' own privacy policies may apply to your connection.

The app stores playlist configuration, EPG mappings, favourites, display preferences, and a per-installation random device identifier only on your computer. The identifier is not a hardware or network identifier.

Wukki TV does not send usage statistics, analytics, or locally stored settings to its own server. It does not provide IPTV content and does not manage user accounts.

When an external stream, EPG, or programme-artwork URL from a playlist is opened, the relevant provider may receive information about your network connection. The user is responsible for using such sources and for the lawful access to their content.

Locally stored app data can be removed by deleting the Wukki TV data directory.
